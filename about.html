Biography

Haotian Zhang is a licensed architect based in New York. His professional experience spans mixed-use development, high-rise architecture, residential projects, adaptive reuse, educational buildings and complex facade systems.

His work is informed by an interest in the relationship between architecture, material systems, geometry and urban context. Alongside architectural practice, he maintains broader interests in photography, automobiles, objects and visual culture.

He received a Master of Science in Advanced Architectural Design from Cornell University and a Bachelor of Architecture from Pratt Institute.
