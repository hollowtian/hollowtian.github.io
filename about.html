Biography

Haotian currently works at SHoP Architects, contributing to projects across multiple scales and typologies, including mixed-use development, residential towers, urban design and adaptive reuse.

His work focuses on the relationship between architectural concept, complex geometry, facade systems and the practical realities of project delivery.

Outside architecture, his interests extend into automobiles, photography, objects, technology and visual culture.
